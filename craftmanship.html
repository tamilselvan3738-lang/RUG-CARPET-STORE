Witness the unhurried kinetic transformation of natural fibers. Over 180 days of relentless human precision, organic highland wool and raw filament silk are spun, botanically dyed, and knotted into generational heirlooms.

01

Alpine Fleece Harvest & Carding

Day 1 — 14

Long-staple wool hand-sheared from high-altitude Himalayan sheep. Guard hairs are extracted by hand, followed by double carding with iron teasel cards to align every filament along its natural crimp.

02

Drop-Spindle Drafting & Botanical Dyeing

Day 15 — 30

Hand-spun into balanced S-twist yarn for maximum tensile strength, then submersed into copper vats of wild indigo, madder root, and walnut husks. Hang-dried under alpine sun.

03

Timber Loom Dressing & Warp Tensioning

Day 31 — 38

Over 8,000 vertical cotton or silk warp threads are strung across heavy seasoned cedarwood looms. Tension is calibrated millimeter-by-millimeter to prevent skewing.

04

Knot-by-Knot Weft Consolidation

Day 39 — 160

Master weavers follow the chanted melodic cipher of the Talim script, wrapping each asymmetrical Persian Senneh knot by hand and packing every row with a forged iron comb.

05

River Washing & Level-Shearing

Day 161 — 180

Vigorous washing with natural walnut foam and mountain spring water, solar tentering on cedar frames, millimeter shear levelling, and the 100-point curator inspection.

Hand Weaving Process & Hand Tufting Process

Hand Weaving Process vs Hand Tufting Process

We master both worlds: the ancient museum discipline of individual knot-tying on vertical timber uprights, and the architectural freedom of sculpted hand-tufting.

Hand Weaving Process

Traditional Hand Weaving Process

Every single knot is individually looped around adjacent warp threads, pulled tight, and cut by hand with a hooked knife. Zero glue, zero backing latex — pure structural thread architecture that lasts 100+ years.

Knot Density (KPSI): 300 to 1,200 Knots / Sq. Inch
Loom Architecture: Vertical Timber Warp Frame
Expected Longevity: Generational (100+ Years)
Backing Material: Natural Warp & Weft (Zero Latex)
Best For:

Formal salons, museum-grade collections, and legacy estates demanding infinite durability.

Hand Tufting Process

Sculptural Hand Tufting Process

Yarn is shot through a primary cotton canvas using a handheld pneumatic needle tool, then anchored with natural organic rubber latex and backed with Belgian linen. Enables multi-level pile heights and sculpted 3D grooves.

Pile Topography: Multi-Level Cut & Loop 3D Relief
Execution Velocity: 4 — 8 Weeks Lead Time
Expected Longevity: 25 — 40 Years of Luxury
Backing Architecture: Natural Hevea Latex & Heavy Linen
Best For:

High-end contemporary penthouses, dramatic bespoke hospitality suites, and custom dimensional contours.

Artisan Techniques & Guild Implements

Forged Steel and Ancient Oral Ciphers

No digital display guides our master weavers. Instead, centuries of codified spoken rhythm guide the fingers. Combined with custom-forged steel tools passed from father to daughter, every knot is struck with unyielding density.

The Heavy Panja (Beater Comb)

Forged from solid iron with up to 14 tempered teeth. Struck rhythmically against the weft line with 35 lbs of downward kinetic force to lock knots into structural rigidity.

The Curved Chhuri & Level Shears

A razor-hook blade that loops through the warp in a fluid 0.8-second motion, accompanied by offset Japanese steel shears calibrated for millimeter leveling.

The Talim Oral Notation Scroll

An encrypted cursive notation resembling musical scores. The master calls out knot counts and hues in a melodic cadence, ensuring symmetrical accuracy across 12-foot looms.

Finishing & Detailing • Quality Inspection

Finishing & Detailing with 100-Point Quality Inspection

A rug is not complete when it leaves the loom. It must undergo meticulous finishing & detailing, surviving 7 rigorous stages and an uncompromising 100-point curator quality inspection before receiving its gold wax seal.

1

Glacial River Wash & Walnut Foam

Scrubbed repeatedly with wooden paddles to purge loose fibers and soften the pile.

2

Solar Cedar Tentering & Blocking

Secured onto dimensional cedar beams under natural sunlight to guarantee true 90° corners.

3

Millimeter Pile Levelling & Carving

Hand-sheared with angled blades to produce flawless pile uniformity and beveled contours.

4

Selvage Overcasting & Fringe Braiding

Borders double-wrapped with goat hair to resist edge curling; warp ends tied into macramé.

5

Optical Gauge KPSI Verification

Brass loupe count verifying knot density uniformity across 10 random grid coordinates.
