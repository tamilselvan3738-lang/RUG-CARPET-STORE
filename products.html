01 • Natural Wax Lanolin High Durability

Wool Rugs

Tibetan highland sheep wool with high lanolin wax content. Inherently stain-resistant, flame-retardant, and exceptionally resilient against compression under heavy furniture.

Best Rooms: Living Room, Great Salons
02 • Long-Staple Warp Crisp Structure

Cotton Rugs

Provides the dimensionally rigid warp and weft skeleton for intricate hand-knotted rugs, as well as lightweight, washable summer flatweaves with natural breathability.

Best Rooms: Breakfast Nooks, Verandas
03 • Golden Bengal Bast Earthy Organic

Jute Rugs

Braided from golden plant bast stalks. Dense, textural, and grounded; provides the quintessential chunky neutral foundation layer for luxury double-rug styling.

Best Rooms: Dining Rooms, Layer Foundations
04 • Mulberry Filament Liquid Luster

Silk Rugs

Continuous natural filament silk with triangular cross-section threads that refract ambient chandelier light with unparalleled opulence and razor-sharp design definition.

Best Rooms: Master Suites, Private Galleries
05 • Bamboo Botanical Mirror Sheen

Viscose Rugs

Regenerated bamboo cellulose fibers producing a silken touch with shimmering directional pile response, commonly blended with wool for high-low sculptural accents.

Best Rooms: Formal Lounges, Executive Suites
06 • Flax, Sisal & Abaca Zero Synthetics

Natural Fiber Rugs

Un-dyed organic plant fibers sourced from Belgian flax fields and sustainable abaca crops. Biodegradable, zero VOC emissions, and organically tactile.

Best Rooms: Corridors, Modern Architecture
