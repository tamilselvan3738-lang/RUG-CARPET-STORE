Longevity Guide

Four Golden Rules for Rug Care

Natural lanolin in sheep wool protects against spills. Follow these simple rules to preserve fiber luster for a lifetime.

1
Brushless Vacuuming Only: Disengage rotating beater bars; gentle suction cleans dust without abrading hand-knotted pile.
2
Annual 180° Solar Rotation: Rotate rugs once a year to ensure even foot traffic distribution and balanced natural light exposure.
3
Immediate Spill Blotting: Blot liquids immediately with a dry white cotton towel. Never scrub or apply chemical detergents.
4
Professional Organic Wash: Have your carpet gently washed every 3 to 5 years by certified heirloom textile conservators.
