Heritage Mastery & Standards

Production Process & Quality Inspection

Six unhurried stages of artisan discipline followed by a rigorous 10-point guild inspection protocol before international white-glove dispatch.

Part 1: Production Process (The 6-Stage Handcrafted Odyssey)
STAGE 01 Week 1 - 2

Architectural Cartoon (Naqsha)

Every single hand knot is hand-painted on millimetric graph cartoons, mapping chromatic codes with surgical exactitude before loom warping.

STAGE 02 Week 2 - 3

Botanical Kettle Dyeing

Raw highland fleece and mulberry silk are boiled in small copper cauldrons with madder root, walnut rinds, and natural mountain spring water.

STAGE 03 Week 3 - 10

Vertical Loom Knotting

Master artisans tie 8,000 to 12,000 individual knots per day by hand. Two to four weavers work in synchronous harmony across the loom span.

STAGE 04 Week 10 - 11

Alpine River Herb Wash

Washed with natural soapnut extract and dried under open Himalayan sunlight, sealing lanolin oils and evoking heirloom depth.

STAGE 05 Week 11 - 12

Artisan Hand-Shearing

Master shearers meticulously sculpt pile heights with heavy curved shears, defining sharp contours and 3D architectural beveling.

STAGE 06 Week 12 - 13

Silk Binding & Guild Seal

Selvedges are reinforced with hand-wound silk cord, knotted fringe secured, and an engraved copper serial guild plate attached.

Optical Loupe Calibrated Verification

Every square decimeter is examined under 10x magnification to confirm knot density tolerance within ±1.5% of architectural specifications.
